Our product
Introducing Velion

A comprehensive clinical operations platform built for independent practices: streamlining appointment scheduling, patient engagement, and practice performance in one integrated system.

Velion
Clinical operations platform

Velion is a full-featured clinical management platform that gives independent practices the tools to manage their entire patient lifecycle: from digital intake and scheduling to automated follow-up, revenue tracking, and performance analytics, all in one place.

  • AI-automated pre and post-visit messaging via SMS and email, with no manual action required from clinic staff
  • Messages personalized to each patient's visit type, care plan, and provider notes
  • Appointment reminder sequences with no-show tracking
  • Digital intake forms patients complete prior to their visit
  • Drag-and-drop appointment calendar
  • Revenue tracking and monthly analytics

Why Velion
Built for the way clinics actually work
01
Built for practices of every size

Most clinical software is designed for large hospital systems: complex, expensive, and difficult to implement. Velion is built specifically for the independent practice that needs enterprise-grade tools without the enterprise overhead.

02
Intelligent, patient-specific communication

Velion tailors every interaction to the individual, adapting to visit type, provider notes, and patient history. The result is timely, relevant communication that strengthens the patient-provider relationship and drives better clinical outcomes.

03
Up and running in under an hour

Velion is built to be used by clinical staff from day one. No lengthy onboarding, no IT consultant, no manual. Most practices are fully operational within the hour, allowing your team to focus on patient care rather than software training.

Our story
Built from direct experience in the clinical setting.

Velion Health was founded on the belief that healthcare must continuously evolve. Guided by a commitment to improving patient outcomes and addressing inefficiencies in existing systems, we develop intelligent, data-driven solutions that advance the standard of modern care.

Independent practices deserve the same quality of tools as large health systems, without the complexity, cost, or implementation burden. Velion was built to close that gap.

1
Scheduling and intake, simplified

Appointments are managed through a drag-and-drop calendar. Digital intake forms are sent to patients before their visit, so staff arrive prepared.

2
Continuity of care between visits

After each appointment, Velion automatically sends a personalized follow-up tailored to the patient's visit type and care plan, keeping them engaged and on track.

3
Patient records, all in one place

Clinical notes, billing details, file attachments, and intake responses are stored in each patient's profile and accessible in seconds.

4
No-shows tracked and addressed proactively

Velion flags patients who have not responded or confirmed, giving staff the visibility to follow up before an appointment is lost.

5
Know exactly how your practice is performing

Revenue trends, message open rates, appointment volume, and no-show data are surfaced in a live analytics dashboard, so decisions are driven by data.
